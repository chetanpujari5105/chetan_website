Sidekick privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Published by Chetan Pujari, 344, Ghadgewasti, MIDC, Baramati – 413133, India · Contact chetanpujari.sitit@gmail.com

What Sidekick is

Two apps, one for your Android phone and one for your Mac, that connect directly to each other on your own network. There is no Sidekick account and no Sidekick server, and we receive nothing from either app.

What moves between your devices

When you turn a feature on, Sidekick moves that feature's data between your phone and your Mac only: notifications, text messages, call events, contacts you look up, clipboard text, files and photos you choose, the list of apps you can open, battery and network status, and your phone's screen while you mirror it. It travels over an encrypted connection (TLS 1.3) whose keys are pinned when you pair the two devices by scanning a code. If you type in an address yourself, for example one on a private network you run, the connection goes over that network instead, encrypted the same way.

What leaves your network

Sidekick's own code sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Neither app contains analytics, advertising, crash reporting or any other service that sends data off your devices. The phone's QR scanner reads the code on the phone itself. If you install Sidekick from Google Play, the copy you receive also includes an installer check that Google Play adds to confirm the app came from Google Play. That check is Google's, and Google's privacy policy covers it.

Control from your Mac (Accessibility)

If you turn on Sidekick Input in Android's Accessibility settings, your paired Mac can tap, swipe, scroll and type on your phone while you mirror its screen. It acts only on what you do on the Mac, never on its own. It reads the text field you have selected, so typing and backspace can change it, and the name of the app in front, so a tap meant for one app never lands in another. It does not read the rest of your screen or watch what you do. Nothing it reads is stored or sent anywhere except your paired Mac, which is told the app's name only when a tap is refused because the app changed. Sidekick asks for your agreement in the app before you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Accessibility.

What is stored, and where

  • On your Mac: files you copy to it (in ~/Downloads/Sidekick, or the folder you choose); an Activity history (event types, times, file names, and call events with the contact's name but never the number), kept to the latest 2,000 events; your beta token (its tester name, id and start and end dates; checked on the Mac and sent nowhere), the last time Sidekick saw the Mac's clock (in your login Keychain, used only to notice a clock set backwards), and which expiry notices were shown; diagnostic logs (connection states, counts and file names, never message, clipboard or file content), at most about 1 MB; your settings and the pairing record; and this Mac's private key, sealed in your login Keychain. All of it is in ~/Library/Application Support/sidekick-desktop except the files you received and the Keychain item.
  • On your phone: files sent to it (in Download/ConnectSuite, or Sidekick's private storage); the pairing record; a transfer history; diagnostic logs of the same kind, at most about 1 MB; and the phone's private key, kept in Android's hardware-backed Keystore, which cannot be exported.
  • Clipboard history is kept in memory only, at most 20 entries, and cleared when the connection ends. Notifications, messages, call lists, contacts and photos shown on the Mac are held in memory while you are connected and are not written to disk, except a photo or file you choose to save.

How long

Until you delete it. Received files stay where you put them. The Activity history keeps the latest 2,000 events. Logs rotate at about 512 KB. The in-memory data above goes when the connection ends or the app quits.

How to delete it

Phone: uninstall Sidekick (files it received stay in Download/ConnectSuite until you delete them). Mac: quit Sidekick, delete it, delete ~/Library/Application Support/sidekick-desktop, and in Keychain Access delete the item named Sidekick Safe Storage. Files you received stay in ~/Downloads/Sidekick until you delete them. Forget on either device removes the pairing.

Backups

The phone app opts out of Android cloud backup. On the Mac, Time Machine includes the folder above unless you exclude it.

Children

Sidekick is not directed at children under 13.
